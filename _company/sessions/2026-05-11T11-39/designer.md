## 🖼️ Visual Prompt 초안: Loss Aversion Hook 기반 시각적 학습 흐름 (Visual Learning Flow)

본 프롬프트는 모든 숏폼 콘텐츠에 적용될 **'High Intensity Warning'** 스타일과 **'#FF8C00'** 컬러 팔레트의 100% 준수를 목표로 합니다. 모션은 **0.5초~1초 이내의 초고속 전환**을 원칙으로 합니다.

### ⚙️ 디자인 시스템 요약 (Master Reference)
*   **브랜드 톤:** 긴급성, 경고, 고효율 (Urgency, Warning, High Intensity)
*   **주요 컬러:** `#FF8C00` (Primary Accent), 어두운 배경 (Dark/Black) 사용.
*   **모션 스타일:** 빠르고 충격적인 전환(Fast & Shocking Transition), 텍스트의 즉각적 강조(Immediate Emphasis).

### 🎯 후크별 시각적 프롬프트 초안

#### **1. 후크: "이것을 모르면, 당신의 모든 것을 잃게 됩니다. 지금 당장 확인하세요."**
*   **목표:** 극도의 공포와 즉각적인 행동 유도 (Fear & Immediate Action)
*   **시각적 프롬프트 초안:**
    > **Visual Concept:** Extreme loss visualization. Start with a close-up shot of the subject looking panicked, then a rapid, jarring zoom into a large, glowing red warning sign superimposed over a rapidly flashing sequence of financial/life-related symbols (e.g., broken graphs, empty wallets). Use heavy motion blur and sharp, aggressive cuts.
    > **Color Focus:** Dominant use of `#FF8C00` for all warning indicators and text overlays. Background should be deep black or dark grey.
    > **Motion Style:** Rapid flicker and jarring transition (0.3s cuts). Text must flash aggressively.

#### **2. 후크: "당신이 지금 겪는 이 실수가 당신의 미래를 완전히 망칠 수 있습니다. (경고!)"**
*   **목표:** 개인적인 책임감 부여 및 위협 강조 (Personal Responsibility & Threat)
*   **시각적 프롬프트 초안:**
    > **Visual Concept:** Focus on a metaphorical representation of future ruin. Start with an image of a path or road that abruptly turns into a cracked, burning landscape. Overlay the text "Your Future Is At Risk" in large, bold, distressed font. Use visual metaphors for failure (e.g., shattering glass, crumbling structure).
    > **Color Focus:** `#FF8C00` used as fire/damage effects and to highlight the word "(경고!)". The transition should be a slow burn that suddenly accelerates into a flash of orange warning.
    > **Motion Style:** Slow build-up of tension followed by an instantaneous, sharp visual shock (Flash Transition).

#### **3. 후크: "당신이 놓치고 있는 단 하나의 핵심 규칙. 이것을 무시하면 큰 손실을 보게 됩니다."**
*   **목표:** FOMO(기회 상실)와 정보의 희소성 강조 (FOMO & Scarcity of Information)
*   **시각적 프롬프트 초안:**
    > **Visual Concept:** Highlight the missing piece. Display a complex, dense structure (representing knowledge) with one single critical element conspicuously missing or obscured. The focus should be on the void left by the missing rule. Use an arrow or pulsating circle to draw attention to the empty spot.
    > **Color Focus:** `#FF8C00` used as a spotlight effect on the missing information and the crucial point. Contrast high-intensity orange against a muted, dark background.
    > **Motion Style:** Slow pan across complex data, then an immediate, sharp focus snap onto the single critical element (Focus Snap Transition).

#### **4. 후크: "당신이 알던 방식은 이제 통하지 않습니다. 낡은 지식 때문에 돈을 잃고 있습니다."**
*   **목표:** 기존 방식에 대한 저항과 변화의 필요성 강조 (Resistance to Change & Frustration)
*   **시각적 프롬프트 초안:**
    > **Visual Concept:** Contrast between an old, broken mechanism/system and a sleek, new, effective solution. Show the "old way" as fragmented or rusted, juxtaposed with the new method appearing clean and powerful. Use visual metaphor of breaking chains or shattering outdated patterns.
    > **Color Focus:** Use muted, dull tones for the 'old' system, contrasting sharply with vibrant `#FF8C00` for the 'new' solution.
    > **Motion Style:** Aggressive disintegration/shattering effect transitioning into a clean, high-speed reveal (Disintegration to Reveal Transition).

#### **5. 후크: "단 3초 만에 당신의 재정/안전을 위협하는 치명적인 오류를 발견하세요."**
*   **목표:** 즉각적 행동(Immediacy)과 충격(Shock) 극대화 (Immediate Action & Shock)
*   **시각적 프롬프트 초안:**
    > **Visual Concept:** Extreme close-up on a screen or data point, showing an immediate, critical error/glitch. The sequence must be extremely fast. Start with static data, then an instantaneous visual glitch effect that transforms the screen into a flashing red alert overlay. A large, ticking countdown timer (3 seconds) must be prominently displayed.
    > **Color Focus:** Maximum saturation of `#FF8C00` for the error message and the timer. Use high-contrast lighting to emphasize the urgency.
    > **Motion Style:** Ultra-fast, high-frequency visual stuttering and immediate screen flash (Stutter & Flash Transition).
