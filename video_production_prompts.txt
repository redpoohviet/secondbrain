## 🎬 영상 콘텐츠 실행용 고해상도 프롬프트 (Visual Learning Flow 적용)

**[디자인 시스템 참조]**
*   **스타일:** High Intensity Warning, 극적인 대비 강조
*   **컬러 팔레트:** #FF8C00 (주요 경고 및 강조), 어두운 배경의 대비 극대화
*   **전환 효과:** Rapid Transition, 고속 화면 전환(Quick Cuts) 사용 필수.
*   **핵심 원칙:** 시각적 흐름(Visual Flow)에 따라 정보가 압축되고 감정적 충격이 최대화되어야 함.

---
### 🎥 영상 1: 금융 보안 문제 (Video_1_Financial_Survival 기반)

**[스크립트 핵심 메시지]**: 베트남인을 위한 금융 보안 문제 및 즉각적인 생존 강조.

**[프롬프트 세트 A - 후크 & 문제 제기 (Hook & Problem)]**
*   **Prompt 1 (Hook):** Extreme close-up shot of a digital lock rapidly shattering with sharp, jagged lines. Background is deep black with intense #FF8C00 glow emanating from the cracks. Cinematic lighting, high contrast, High Intensity Warning style. Focus on immediate threat and fear.
*   **Prompt 2 (Problem Setup):** Abstract visualization of chaotic, tangled financial documents dissolving into digital static. Motion graphic overlay showing a massive red warning symbol flashing rapidly across the screen. Rapid transition effect. Deep shadows, sharp edges, saturated orange tone.
*   **Prompt 3 (Urgency):** Animated graphic sequence: A timeline rapidly collapsing, illustrating lost opportunities and impending financial disaster. Use aggressive motion lines and strobe effects in #FF8C00. Visual flow emphasizes irreversible loss.

**[프롬프트 세트 B - 해결책 제시 & CTA]**
*   **Prompt 4 (Solution Intro):** Seamless transition to a clean, stark visual of a single, secure digital vault icon glowing intensely with #FF8C00 light. The background shifts from chaos to controlled order. High-resolution, cinematic depth of field.
*   **Prompt 5 (App CTA):** Bold, centered text overlay in white and bright orange: "STOP THE LOSS. Secure Your Future NOW." The text should pulse slightly with the #FF8C00 color. Include a subtle visual arrow pointing towards an imaginary call-to-action space.

### 🎥 영상 2 & 3: 중국어 학습 및 실생활 문제 해결 (숏폼 시리즈 기반)

**[스크립트 핵심 메시지]**: 생존/문제 해결 효용성 극대화, 사람 없이 모션 그래픽 중심.

**[프롬프트 세트 C - 상황 제시 & 경고 (Situation & Warning)]**
*   **Prompt 6 (Scenario Setup):** Abstract visualization of a complex, confusing maze or tangled path representing a difficult real-life scenario (e.g., financial confusion). Use dark blue and harsh orange lines to define the boundaries. Motion graphic focus.
*   **Prompt 7 (Danger Highlight):** Dynamic visual representation of critical error messages flashing in Chinese characters (or universal warning symbols) overlaid with aggressive #FF8C00 text warnings. Visual flow demands immediate attention and recognition of danger. Rapid motion.

**[프롬프트 세트 D - 학습/해결 과정 & 결과 (Learning & Result)]**
*   **Prompt 8 (The Shift):** Visual transition: The chaotic maze instantly resolves into a clear, organized path illuminated by bright gold light (#FF8C00). Clean geometric shapes and smooth, controlled motion. This represents the learning process.
*   **Prompt 9 (Outcome & CTA):** Final frame showing a secure, successful outcome graphic (e.g., a completed checklist or solved equation) with a strong, bold CTA: "Master Survival Language. Download Now." Use the established color palette for maximum impact.
